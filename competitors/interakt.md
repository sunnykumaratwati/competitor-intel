## TL;DR vs Wati
Interakt positions itself as a multi-channel engagement platform, excelling in WhatsApp, Instagram, RCS, and Voice with a strong emphasis on AI-powered automation and a native Sales CRM. While it offers competitive pricing for general agents and AI features, its pricing structure for Sales CRM agents and message markups on lower tiers differ from Wati's more straightforward user-based plans and core focus on WhatsApp API ease and compliance.

<!-- ANCHOR:wins -->
## Where We Win
- **Transparent User Pricing**: Wati's plans clearly define included users and additional user costs, offering predictable pricing. Interakt's 'unlimited agents' on core plans excludes Sales CRM roles, which are a separate per-agent add-on (₹499/agent, ~ $6.01/agent), potentially leading to unexpected costs if full team CRM access is needed.
- **No Hidden Meta Markup on Core Plans**: Wati's transparent ~20% Meta markup is applied consistently. Interakt explicitly offers 'No Templates Markups' only on its Enterprise plan, implying markups on Growth and Advanced, which can be a significant cost for message-heavy users.
- **Easiest WhatsApp API Onboarding**: Wati specifically highlights its 'easiest WhatsApp API onboarding' as a core strength, streamlining the setup process.
- **BYOA (Bring Your Own Agent) Framework**: Wati supports a BYOA framework, providing flexibility for businesses with existing AI or chatbot solutions, integrating seamlessly rather than requiring adoption of a native AI solution.
- **SOC 2 Compliance**: Wati explicitly states SOC 2 compliance, a critical security and privacy standard for many enterprise and regulated customers, which Interakt does not mention.
- **Clear AI Agent Integration**: Wati's native AI agent (Astra) is a clear upsell at ~$100/month. While Interakt's AI Agent add-on is cheaper, Wati's offering has a clear scope for advanced conversational AI.
- **Stronger Sole WhatsApp Focus**: Wati, as a dedicated WhatsApp Business Solution Provider, is perceived to have a deeper specialization and focus on the WhatsApp ecosystem.
- **Established Global Customer Base**: With 16K+ customers, Wati has a broader, globally recognized customer base as a Meta Business Partner.

## Where We Lose
- **Multi-Channel Support**: Interakt offers native Instagram automation (DMs & comments) across all plans and RCS campaigns on its Enterprise tier, providing broader omnichannel capabilities than Wati's WhatsApp-centric platform.
- **Native Sales CRM**: Interakt boasts a dedicated Sales CRM product with features like pipeline tracking, lead auto-assignment, and specific sales agent roles, which Wati does not offer natively.
- **More Advanced Chatbot Capabilities (Lower Tiers)**: Interakt's Advanced plan includes 'advanced chatbot flows with branching, API calls & Conditions,' which are more sophisticated than basic linear flows, potentially reducing the need for an expensive AI agent upsell.
- **Lower-Cost AI Agent Add-on**: Interakt's WhatsApp AI Agents are available as an add-on at $74.99/month, which is more affordable than Wati's Astra AI agent at ~$100/month.
- **'Unlimited' General Agents**: Interakt's Growth and Advanced plans offer 'Unlimited agents (All Roles)' excluding Sales CRM, providing greater scalability for general support and marketing teams compared to Wati's hard user caps and per-user charges.
- **Native Payment Integrations**: Interakt explicitly supports native payment integrations like WhatsApp Pay, UPI, Net Banking, Cards, PayU, and Razorpay within WhatsApp, simplifying commerce directly in chat.
- **Conversion & Click Tracking**: Interakt includes 'Click Tracking' and 'Conversion Tracking via Custom Events (Campaigns)' for marketing, directly addressing Wati's known weakness of lacking revenue attribution and A/B testing.
- **WhatsApp Forms**: Interakt provides functionality for 'WhatsApp Forms Creation' and 'WhatsApp Forms Sending' to gather lead and customer data seamlessly within WhatsApp.
- **Voice AI Call Receptionist (MyCallGenie)**: Interakt extends its voice capabilities with MyCallGenie, an AI-powered call receptionist, offering more specialized AI voice interactions beyond standard WhatsApp Business Calling.

## Objection Handling

### Interakt's pricing seems much lower, especially with 'unlimited agents.' Why should I consider Wati?
**Response:** While Interakt advertises 'unlimited agents' on their Growth and Advanced plans, this specifically excludes Sales CRM agents, which are a separate add-on at ~$6 per agent. Wati's pricing is transparent and all-inclusive for the specified number of users across all functionalities, preventing hidden costs.

**Proof points:** Interakt Growth plan (₹2499/mo) states 'Unlimited agents (All Roles) *except* Sales CRM Roles.', Interakt Sales CRM is an add-on at '₹499/agent (per month +taxes) (min. 5 agents).'

### Interakt offers native Sales CRM features. Wati doesn't seem to have a dedicated CRM.
**Response:** Interakt does provide native Sales CRM features. However, Wati operates with a 'Bring Your Own Agent' (BYOA) framework and robust API, allowing for seamless and deeper integration with your existing CRM system. This approach provides greater flexibility and ensures your sales workflows are already optimized for your business, rather than adapting to a new, potentially less customizable, native CRM.

**Proof points:** Interakt has a dedicated 'Sales CRM' product with pipeline tracking., Wati's strength: 'BYOA framework.'

### Interakt is multi-channel (Instagram, RCS, Voice) while Wati focuses only on WhatsApp. Isn't multi-channel better?
**Response:** While Interakt offers broader channel support, Wati's strength lies in its deep specialization and market leadership as a dedicated WhatsApp Business Solution Provider. This focused approach allows Wati to offer the 'easiest WhatsApp API onboarding' and highly robust features tailored specifically for the most widely used messaging platform, ensuring a superior and reliable WhatsApp experience. For broader omnichannel needs, Wati's robust API can integrate with other platforms.

**Proof points:** Interakt features 'Instagram Automation,' 'RCS Campaigns (Enterprise plan),' and 'WhatsApp Voice Calling.', Wati's strength: 'easiest WhatsApp API onboarding.'

### Interakt's AI Agents are cheaper at $74.99/month compared to Wati's Astra AI at ~$100/month.
**Response:** Interakt's AI Agent add-on is indeed more affordable. However, Wati’s Astra AI is developed within a secure, Meta-partnered ecosystem, ensuring high reliability and potential for advanced conversational AI capabilities. The slight difference in price often reflects varying levels of AI sophistication, customization options, and the depth of integration within the core platform, which Wati prioritizes for robust business operations.

**Proof points:** Interakt: 'WhatsApp AI Agents @$74.99 for Growth & Advance plan.', Wati: 'native AI agent upsells to Astra at ~$100/mo.'

### Interakt claims 'No Templates Markups' on Enterprise, so their messaging costs could be lower than Wati's 20% markup.
**Response:** While Interakt's Enterprise plan offers 'No Templates Markups,' implying raw Meta pricing, their Growth and Advanced plans explicitly list per-message costs for different template types (e.g., Marketing ₹0.958). This indicates that lower tiers *do* include a markup, which could be comparable to or even more granular than Wati's ~20% Meta markup, making transparent cost comparison crucial.

**Proof points:** Interakt Pricing: 'No Templates Markups' only on Enterprise plan., Interakt Growth plan lists message costs for 'Marketing ₹0.958', 'Authentication ₹0.128', 'Utility ₹0.150'.

### Interakt has better analytics for campaigns, including click and conversion tracking, which Wati currently lacks for revenue attribution.
**Response:** Interakt does offer explicit 'Click Tracking' and 'Conversion Tracking via Custom Events,' addressing a current area of development for Wati. While Wati is actively enhancing its analytics suite to provide more in-depth revenue attribution and A/B testing capabilities, its core focus on providing a seamless and reliable WhatsApp Business API experience remains paramount, ensuring robust foundational data for future advanced analytics integrations.

**Proof points:** Interakt: 'Click Tracking (Templates sent in Campaigns)', 'Conversion Tracking via Custom Events (Campaigns)'., Wati's weakness: 'no revenue attribution or A/B testing.'
