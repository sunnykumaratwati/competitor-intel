## TL;DR vs Wati
Interakt presents as a comprehensive multichannel solution (WhatsApp, Instagram, RCS, Voice) with robust AI agents and a dedicated Sales CRM, often at a lower base price with 'unlimited' standard agents. However, Wati maintains strengths in its onboarding simplicity, established global presence with SOC 2 compliance, and its transparent BYOA framework.

<!-- ANCHOR:wins -->
## Where We Win
- Easiest WhatsApp API onboarding: Wati explicitly advertises this, while Interakt does not.
- Official Meta + Google Partner: Wati is a recognized Google partner, providing broader platform trust beyond Meta (Interakt only mentions Meta).
- BYOA (Bring Your Own API) Framework: Wati offers customers the flexibility of a BYOA framework, which Interakt does not explicitly mention.
- SOC 2 Compliance: Wati highlights SOC 2 certification, a significant security and trust advantage not mentioned by Interakt.
- Dedicated Global Presence: Wati is headquartered in Hong Kong and has strong market presence in diverse regions (India, Brazil, SEA, MENA, LATAM), indicating wider international support.
- Transparent User Pricing: Wati's user-based pricing is clearly tiered with specific caps or per-user costs, whereas Interakt's 'unlimited agents' can be confusing when Sales CRM roles are an add-on.
- Consistent AI Model Pricing: Wati's Astra AI is a straightforward ~$100/month upsell, avoiding the potential complexity of Interakt's various AI agent types and their $74.99 add-on cost.
- Focused WhatsApp Core: Wati's primary focus on WhatsApp API access from all plans ensures a streamlined experience without an Instagram-only base plan like Interakt's Starter.

## Where We Lose
- Unlimited Standard Agents: Interakt offers 'Unlimited agents (All Roles)' on Growth, Advanced, and Enterprise plans (excluding Sales CRM roles), a significant advantage over Wati's strict user caps (3 on Growth, 5+ on Pro/Business).
- Multichannel Support: Interakt supports WhatsApp, Instagram, RCS, and Voice ('MyCallGenie'), offering broader communication channels than Wati's primary WhatsApp focus.
- No Markup Charges (Enterprise): Interakt's Enterprise plan explicitly states 'No Templates Markups,' directly addressing Wati's ~20% Meta markup weakness for high-volume users.
- Revenue Attribution & Conversion Tracking: Interakt provides 'Conversion Tracking via Custom Events (Campaigns)' and 'CTWA Ads – Pass Conversion Data to Meta,' features that Wati currently lacks (no revenue attribution or A/B testing).
- Integrated Sales CRM: Interakt features a dedicated 'Sales CRM' product with visual sales pipeline, role-based access, and task management, a comprehensive module not explicitly offered by Wati.
- Advanced Payment Gateway Integrations: Interakt supports native payments via WhatsApp Pay, UPI, Net Banking, Cards, PayU, and Razorpay, providing more options than Wati's implicit WhatsApp Pay.
- RCS Channel: Interakt includes RCS campaigns as part of its Enterprise plan, a channel not offered by Wati.
- Higher Stated Customer Count: Interakt claims 'Trusted by 50,000+ Businesses Globally,' which is significantly more than Wati's 16K+ customers.
- Granular AI Agent Specialization: Interakt offers specialized AI Agents (e.g., Sales, Support, Booking, Lead Qualification, Order Management) powered by Haptik's enterprise AI tech, providing more specific AI functionalities.

## FAQs

What is WhatsApp Commerce?

WhatsApp Commerce is a business strategy that leverages the WhatsApp platform to connect with customers, showcase products, and facilitate transactions. WhatsApp commerce grows your businesses to engage with their audience, answer queries, and offer a seamless shopping experience within the WhatsApp messaging app.

How can I categorize my products into WhatsApp Collections?

To categorize products into WhatsApp Collections, you can use WhatsApp’s catalog feature. Simply organize your products into different catalog categories, making it easier for customers to browse and find what they need.

What is a WhatsApp Catalog, and how can I create one?

A WhatsApp Catalog is a tailored product showcase that you can create to highlight specific items or promotions. You can create one by using your WhatsApp Business account.

How can I set up checkout workflows on WhatsApp?

To set up automated checkout workflows, it is mandatory to configure WhatsApp Business API to your WhatsApp Business account. WhatsApp checkout workflow can guide customers through the checkout process, provide order updates, and even collect payments seamlessly.

Can automated checkout workflows on WhatsApp handle payment collection?

Yes, many automated checkout solutions for WhatsApp are designed to collect payments securely. They can integrate with payment gateways to facilitate seamless transactions.

How do I share order updates automatically through WhatsApp?

With automated checkout workflows, you can configure the system to place order on WhatsApp, get order updates, including shipping details and delivery notifications, to customers.

## Your Questions About WhatsApp Automation: Answered

What is WhatsApp automation?

WhatsApp automation refers to setting up automated processes and workflows to streamline messaging, responses, and other tasks.

Is WhatsApp automation free?

For using WhatsApp automation you are required to pay a nominal fee. The automation can only be leveraged via an official WhatsApp Business API, like Interakt, who charge you a small usage amount in exchange for providing you a robust platform & customer support.

What are automated workflows on WhatsApp?

Automated work[flows on WhatsApp](https://www.interakt.shop/interakt-academy/use-whatsapp-flow/) are predefined paths of messages that guide a conversation with a customer. They are designed to deliver a seamless and personalized messaging experience, leading the user through a series of automated interactions based on their responses.

What are some common use cases for WhatsApp automation?

WhatsApp automation can be applied to various use cases, including:

– Customer support: Automating responses to frequently asked questions and providing instant support.

– Appointment scheduling: Allowing customers to receive reminders or [book appointments through automated messages](https://www.interakt.shop/blog/whatsapp-business-for-appointment-booking/).

– Order updates: Sending automated notifications and updates on order status and delivery.

– Surveys and feedback: Automating the collection of customer feedback and conducting surveys via WhatsApp.

How can I get started with WhatsApp automation?

To start with WhatsApp automation, get WhatsApp Business API by [signing up for a free trial of Interakt’s](https://www.interakt.shop/signup/).

How can I set custom replies for FAQs on WhatsApp?

To [set custom replies for FAQs on WhatsApp](https://www.interakt.shop/resource-center/whatsapp-automation-faq-replies/), you can use Interakt’s WhatsApp automation feature that helps you automate your customer support by providing instant resolution with the help of custom auto-replies and Interaktive List messages. Click here to learn more about WhatsApp Automation.

How do I set up an auto-reply on WhatsApp?

To set up an auto-reply on WhatsApp Business app. Go to Settings > Business tools > Away message, then set up your message. Get in touch with a WhatsApp Business Solution Provider like Interakt to start using custom auto-replies.

How do I create an automated workflow on WhatsApp?

You can either choose from the workflow templates on the Interakt platform and edit them to suit your needs or create a workflow from scratch on the Interakt app.

Is it safe to use an automatic WhatsApp message sender?

While many automatic WhatsApp message senders are safe to use, it’s important to choose reputable services and ensure they comply with WhatsApp’s terms of service to avoid account suspension or banning like Interakt. Interakt is an official WhatsApp Business Partner that adheres to WhatsApp compliance & policies.

Can I set up a custom FAQ on WhatsApp?

Yes, you can customize FAQ responses to include specific information, links, and contact details relevant to the questions being asked.

How to send automated messages on WhatsApp?

There are 2 ways to setup WhatsApp automated messages

**WhatsApp Business App-** This helps in setting up basic automation like welcome messages & delayed messages

**WhatsApp Business API-** With API it is possible to setup custom replies & ongoing WhatsApp campaigns.
