## TL;DR vs Wati
Interakt is a multi-channel customer engagement platform with robust WhatsApp and Instagram features, including advanced AI agents and a native Sales CRM. While it offers competitive pricing for entry-level plans and no Meta markup for enterprise messaging, Wati maintains an advantage in transparent WhatsApp-centric onboarding, explicit compliance (SOC 2), and flexibility through its BYOA framework.
<!-- /ANCHOR:tldr -->

<!-- ANCHOR:wins -->
## Where We Win
- Wati offers the easiest WhatsApp API onboarding, a core strength not explicitly matched by Interakt's content.
- Wati is an official Google Partner in addition to being a Meta Partner; Interakt only highlights its Meta Business Partner status.
- Wati explicitly states SOC 2 compliance, providing a critical trust and security advantage for enterprise clients, which Interakt does not mention.
- Wati supports a BYOA (Bring Your Own Account) framework, offering greater flexibility in account management, a feature not mentioned by Interakt.
- Wati's user-based pricing for mid-market plans (Pro $119, Business $279) is straightforward and predictable, whereas Interakt's 'unlimited agents' come with caveats and add-ons for specific roles like Sales CRM.
- Wati's focused WhatsApp-first approach ensures a streamlined experience for businesses primarily using WhatsApp, potentially reducing feature bloat compared to Interakt's broader multi-channel offering.
<!-- /ANCHOR:wins -->

<!-- ANCHOR:losses -->
## Where We Lose
- Interakt offers comprehensive multi-channel support including WhatsApp, Instagram (DMs and comments), RCS, and Voice, which extends beyond Wati's primary WhatsApp focus.
- Interakt provides a suite of advanced AI Agents (Sales, Support, Booking, etc.) as an integrated add-on ($74.99/mo) with no-code setup and training on custom data, contrasting with Wati's higher-priced (~$100/mo) Astra AI upsell.
- Interakt features a native Sales CRM with visual pipeline tracking, lead qualification, and auto-assignment, a capability Wati lacks natively.
- Interakt's Enterprise plan explicitly states 'No Templates Markups' and 'Unlimited Messages', directly addressing Wati's ~20% Meta markup weakness for high-volume messaging.
- Interakt offers built-in campaign performance tracking with 'Click Tracking (Templates sent in Campaigns)' and 'Conversion Tracking via Custom Events (Campaigns)', features Wati explicitly lacks for revenue attribution and A/B testing.
- Interakt supports richer interactive message templates including Carousels, Limited Time Offers, WhatsApp Forms, and WhatsApp Pay integration across various plans, offering more sophisticated marketing options.
- Interakt provides robust Instagram automation for DMs, 'Price Please' comments, and story mention replies, a significant feature absent from Wati's offerings.
- Interakt clearly outlines 'Outbound Calling from Inbox' availability in its Advanced and Enterprise plans, offering a transparent tiered approach to voice features.

## Feature Notes (from their docs)
- Interakt's AI Agents are an add-on priced at $74.99/month for Growth and Advanced plans (Pricing page).
- The Sales CRM functionality is provided via an add-on plan costing ₹499/agent/month (+taxes) with a minimum of 5 agents, separate from the core platform's 'unlimited agents' (Sales CRM page, Pricing page).

- Omnichannel support expands with plans: WhatsApp only for Starter, WhatsApp & Instagram for Growth/Advanced, and WhatsApp, Instagram & RCS for Enterprise (Pricing page).

## Objection Handling

### Interakt has a fully integrated Sales CRM with pipeline management; Wati doesn't offer this.
**Response:** While Interakt offers a native Sales CRM, Wati focuses on providing robust API integrations, allowing businesses to seamlessly connect with their existing CRM systems. This provides greater flexibility and avoids vendor lock-in for your CRM solution.

**Proof points:** Interakt's Sales CRM is an add-on at ₹499/agent/month (min. 5 agents)., Wati emphasizes a BYOA framework and broad integration capabilities (implied for CRM).

### Interakt offers multi-channel support for Instagram and RCS, which Wati lacks.
**Response:** Wati specializes in deep and highly optimized WhatsApp integration, leveraging its status as an official Meta and Google partner to ensure top-tier WhatsApp-specific features and reliability. While Interakt offers multi-channel, Wati provides unparalleled stability and features for businesses focused on the WhatsApp ecosystem.

**Proof points:** Interakt supports WhatsApp, Instagram, and RCS (Enterprise plan) (Homepage, Pricing)., Wati is an official Meta + Google partner, ensuring deep WhatsApp expertise and integrations (Wati context).

### Interakt's AI Agents seem more comprehensive and affordable than Wati's AI offerings.
**Response:** Interakt does offer a range of AI agents as an add-on, but Wati's native AI agent, Astra, is designed for advanced conversational AI and continuously evolving for complex use cases. Wati's focus on easy WhatsApp API onboarding also means quicker deployment and integration for its AI features.

**Proof points:** Interakt's AI Agents are an add-on at $74.99/month for Growth and Advanced plans (Pricing page)., Wati's native AI agent upsells to Astra at ~$100/mo (Wati weakness).

### Interakt offers 'No Markup Charges' on Enterprise plans, unlike Wati's ~20% Meta markup.
**Response:** Wati transparently includes Meta's markup as part of its cost structure, but it balances this with an industry-leading, easiest WhatsApp API onboarding and robust support. For many businesses, the speed of deployment and reliable service can offset message cost differences, especially when considering total cost of ownership.

**Proof points:** Interakt's Enterprise plan explicitly states 'No Templates Markups' (Pricing page)., Wati notes a ~20% Meta markup as a weakness (Wati context).

### Interakt provides conversion tracking and click tracking for campaigns, which Wati doesn't.
**Response:** While Wati currently lacks native revenue attribution and A/B testing features, its open API and BYOA framework empower customers to integrate with preferred third-party analytics and tracking tools. This flexibility ensures businesses can build a bespoke analytics stack tailored to their specific needs.

**Proof points:** Interakt offers 'Conversion Tracking via Custom Events (Campaigns)' and 'Click Tracking (Templates sent in Campaigns)' (Pricing page, Marketing Hub)., Wati lacks revenue attribution or A/B testing (Wati weakness).

### Interakt claims 'unlimited agents' on most plans, while Wati has a 3-user cap on Growth.
**Response:** Interakt does offer 'unlimited agents' for general roles, but this comes with a crucial caveat: Sales CRM roles require a separate, per-agent add-on. Wati provides clear, predictable per-user pricing for its Pro and Business plans, ensuring transparent costs for scaling teams with diverse functional needs.

**Proof points:** Interakt's Growth/Advanced/Enterprise plans include 'Unlimited agents (All Roles)' but note 'Sales CRM Roles not included' (Pricing page)., Interakt's Sales CRM is ₹499/agent/month (min. 5 agents) (Sales CRM page); Wati's Growth plan has a '3 users, hard cap' (Wati context).
