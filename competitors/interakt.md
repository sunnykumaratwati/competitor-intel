## TL;DR vs Wati
Interakt is a multi-channel customer engagement platform specializing in WhatsApp, Instagram, and RCS, offering a strong focus on AI Agents, a dedicated Sales CRM, and an 'unlimited agents' model for its core support and marketing hub. While cheaper on base plans with broader channel support, it has varied message markups and separate pricing for its Sales CRM and AI capabilities.
<!-- /ANCHOR:tldr -->

<!-- ANCHOR:wins -->
## Where We Win
- Official Google Partner: Wati is an 'official Meta + Google partner', providing broader strategic alliances compared to Interakt's 'Meta Business Partners' status.
- BYOA Framework: Wati offers a 'BYOA framework' for greater flexibility in integrating customer's existing AI or custom solutions, whereas Interakt's AI is powered by its parent company Haptik's proprietary technology.
- SOC 2 Compliance: Wati is SOC 2 compliant, providing a recognized enterprise-grade security certification not explicitly mentioned by Interakt in the provided content.
- Easiest WhatsApp API Onboarding: Wati is known for its 'easiest WhatsApp API onboarding', which streamlines the initial setup process for businesses.
<!-- /ANCHOR:wins -->

<!-- ANCHOR:losses -->
## Where We Lose
- Multi-Channel Support: Interakt supports WhatsApp, Instagram, RCS, and Voice (via MyCallGenie AI Call Receptionist), offering a broader omnichannel presence than Wati's primary focus on WhatsApp.
- User Capacity & Cost Efficiency: Interakt's Growth and Advanced plans offer 'Unlimited agents (All Roles)' for its core Marketing & Support Hub (starting ~$23.94/mo annual), significantly more flexible and cheaper than Wati's Growth plan's 3-user hard cap ($59/mo) or per-user charges on Pro/Business plans.
- Dedicated Sales CRM: Interakt provides a full-fledged 'Sales CRM' product with features like sales pipeline tracking, lead auto-assignment, and WhatsApp Payments, which is a more robust sales-specific offering than Wati's general team inbox.
- AI Agent Affordability: Interakt's AI Agents are available as an add-on for $74.99/mo for Growth & Advanced plans, which is more affordable than Wati's native AI agent upsell to Astra at ~$100/mo.
- Conversion & Click Tracking: Interakt offers 'Click Tracking (Templates sent in Campaigns)' and 'Conversion Tracking via Custom Events (Campaigns)', functionalities Wati explicitly lacks.
- Advanced Chatbot Capabilities: Interakt's Advanced plan includes 'advanced chatbot flows with branching, API calls & Conditions', enabling more complex, conditional automation workflows.
- No Message Markup on Enterprise: Interakt's Enterprise plan explicitly states 'No Templates Markups', a direct contrast to Wati's stated '~20% Meta markup (highest)'.
- WhatsApp Forms: Interakt provides explicit features for 'WhatsApp Forms Creation' and offers various methods for sending these forms across Growth, Advanced, and Enterprise plans.
- Outbound Calling from Inbox: Interakt's Advanced and Enterprise plans feature 'Outbound Calling from Inbox', enhancing agent productivity within the platform.

## Feature Notes (from their docs)
- Interakt's core plans (Growth, Advanced) offer 'Unlimited agents (All Roles)' for the Marketing & Support Hub, but Sales CRM roles are separate and charged at ~$4.78/agent/month (annual).
- The AI Agents feature is an add-on at $74.99/month for Growth & Advanced plans.
- Interakt's listed message costs (e.g., Marketing ₹0.958, Authentication ₹0.128 for Growth) include their markup, which is significant compared to Meta's base rates. However, the Enterprise plan explicitly offers 'No Markup Charges'.
- Custom fields, tags, and events have varying limits across plans, with the Enterprise plan offering 'Unlimited Custom Fields, Unlimited Custom Tags, Unlimited Custom Events'.
- Advanced chatbot flows, including branching, API calls, and conditions, are available from the Advanced plan onwards.
- Interakt's pricing page is titled 'Pricing – US' but displays prices in Indian Rupees (₹), requiring conversion for direct USD comparison. (Conversion: 1 USD = 83.5 INR used for analysis).
- Outbound Calling from Inbox is a feature available on Advanced and Enterprise plans.
- Interakt offers Instagram Automation, including quick-flows for 'price please' queries and giveaway campaigns, alongside a shared team inbox for Instagram DMs and comments.
- Interakt includes specific interactive template types like Carousel and WhatsApp Pay integration from the Growth plan upwards.

**Proof points:** Interakt AI add-on: $74.99/mo., Wati Astra AI agent upsells at ~$100/mo.

### Interakt offers conversion and click tracking for campaigns, which Wati doesn't.

Our focus remains on offering the easiest WhatsApp API onboarding and a reliable core platform, ensuring foundational success while we enhance analytics.

**Proof points:** Interakt: 'Click Tracking (Templates sent in Campaigns)', 'Conversion Tracking via Custom Events (Campaigns)', Wati weakness: 'no revenue attribution or A/B testing'

### Interakt has a full Sales CRM built into its platform.

This approach allows businesses to leverage their preferred CRM for sales pipeline management while benefiting from Wati's powerful WhatsApp engagement tools, avoiding vendor lock-in for a complete CRM overhaul.

**Proof points:** Interakt: 'Sales CRM Boost Sales with WhatsApp CRM', 'Sales Pipeline Tracking', 'Leads auto-assignment'., Wati: 'BYOA framework', 'WhatsApp Team Inbox'.

### Interakt's Enterprise plan has 'No Templates Markups', unlike Wati's 20% Meta markup.
**Response:** While Wati has a ~20% Meta markup on messages, our pricing model is transparent and includes comprehensive features and support. For enterprise clients, Wati focuses on delivering a lower total cost of ownership through simplified onboarding, platform stability, and dedicated support, which often outweighs minor differences in per-message costs for overall operational efficiency.

**Proof points:** Interakt Enterprise: 'No Templates Markups'., Wati weakness: '~20% Meta markup (highest)'.

### Still Have Questions? We’ve Got Answers

What is marketing automation?

Marketing automation is the use of AI and software tools like Interakt to automate repetitive tasks such as email marketing, WhatsApp campaigns, and lead nurturing.

What are the top marketing automation tools?

Top marketing automation tools include Interakt, HubSpot, Marketo, ActiveCampaign, and Mailchimp, helping businesses automate customer interactions.

What are the ways to use marketing automation tools?

Marketing automation tools can be used for email marketing, WhatsApp automation, lead nurturing, customer segmentation, and personalized messaging.

Can AI be used in marketing automation?

Yes, AI in marketing automation helps optimize campaigns, personalize customer interactions, and analyze engagement data to improve marketing ROI.

What is the difference between email marketing and marketing automation?

Email marketing focuses on sending bulk emails, while marketing automation manages customer journeys using AI-powered workflows across multiple channels.

What are the types of marketing automation?

Types of marketing automation include email automation, chatbot marketing, WhatsApp automation, lead scoring, and retargeting campaigns.
