## TL;DR vs Wati
Interakt offers a multi-channel customer engagement platform (WhatsApp, Instagram, RCS, Voice) with integrated Marketing, Support, and Sales CRM solutions, alongside specialized AI agents. It positions itself as a more affordable and feature-rich option compared to Wati, particularly for comprehensive sales and marketing automation and broader channel reach, though its pricing structure can be less transparent due to currency conversion and modular add-ons.
<!-- /ANCHOR:tldr -->

<!-- ANCHOR:wins -->
## Where We Win
- Pricing Transparency: Wati's pricing is clearly stated in USD per month with explicit user costs. Interakt's base pricing is in INR, with a USD add-on for AI, creating potential confusion for US-based buyers.
- Meta Markup Transparency: Wati is upfront about its ~20% Meta markup. Interakt implies markups on its Growth and Advanced plans through listed message costs, only guaranteeing 'no markup' on its Enterprise plan.
- SOC 2 Compliance: Wati highlights SOC 2 compliance, a critical security accreditation. Interakt does not mention any security certifications on its public pages.
- Official Google Partner: Wati is an official Google partner, providing additional credibility and integration confidence not mentioned by Interakt.
- BYOA Framework: Wati supports a 'Bring Your Own Assistant' (BYOA) framework for AI, offering flexibility for businesses with existing AI models or preferences.
- Streamlined WhatsApp API Onboarding: Wati emphasizes 'easiest WhatsApp API onboarding' as a key strength, suggesting a simpler initial setup process.
- Clear User Pricing: Wati's user limits and pricing for additional users are explicit and easy to understand (e.g., Growth $59 for 3 users, hard cap). Interakt's 'unlimited agents' is qualified by extra costs for Sales CRM roles.
- Consistent Mobile App Experience: While Wati's Android app is noted as sluggish, the overall stability and long-term user experience of its mobile applications are established, whereas Interakt provides no specifics on mobile app performance.
<!-- /ANCHOR:wins -->

<!-- ANCHOR:losses -->
## Where We Lose
- Multi-Channel Support: Interakt offers a unified inbox and automation across WhatsApp, Instagram (DMs & comments), and RCS, providing a more comprehensive omnichannel solution than Wati's primary WhatsApp focus.
- Dedicated Sales CRM: Interakt features a full-fledged Sales CRM with pipeline tracking, lead auto-assignment, custom permissions, and WhatsApp Payments, functionalities not native to Wati.
- RCS Campaigns: Interakt supports Rich Communication Services (RCS) campaigns for richer, branded messaging experiences, a channel not directly offered by Wati.
- Advanced Marketing Analytics: Interakt provides granular 'Click Tracking' and 'Conversion Tracking via Custom Events (Campaigns)' for deeper campaign optimization, addressing Wati's noted weakness in revenue attribution and A/B testing.
- Native Payment Integrations: Interakt offers 'Native payments' and direct integrations with WhatsApp Pay, UPI, Net Banking, Cards, PayU, and Razorpay within WhatsApp, simplifying in-chat commerce.
- Sophisticated Chatbot Capabilities: Interakt provides 'advanced chatbot flows with branching, API calls & Conditions' and 'AI-generated workflows and AI-based intent detection' in its no-code builder, more advanced than basic linear flows.
- Specialized AI Agents: Interakt offers distinct AI agents pre-built for specific functions like Customer Support, Booking, Lead Qualification, Sales, Product Recommendation, and Order Management, trained for tailored interactions.
- Potential for 'Unlimited' Agents: For non-Sales CRM roles, Interakt's Growth and Advanced plans advertise 'Unlimited agents,' which can be more cost-effective for larger teams focusing on support or marketing compared to Wati's tiered per-user pricing.
- WhatsApp Forms: Interakt explicitly highlights 'WhatsApp Forms' creation and sending for lead generation and data collection, a detailed feature not as prominently featured or tiered by Wati.
- Campaign Auto-retries: Interakt includes 'Campaign Auto-retries' to ensure message delivery, a specific automation feature for improved marketing campaign success.

## Feature Notes (from their docs)
- No Public Documentation: Key documentation pages (e.g., /docs, /features, /customers) returned 404 errors, making it impossible to ascertain specific API behaviors, advanced limits, or detailed technical specifications beyond marketing claims.
- Tiered Chatbot Functionality: Basic linear chatbot flows are available in the Growth plan, while advanced flows with branching, API calls, and conditions are restricted to the Advanced plan and above.
- AI Agents as Add-on: WhatsApp AI Agents are an additional cost of $74.99/month, applicable to Growth and Advanced plans, meaning they are not included in the base subscription price.
- Sales CRM Agent Pricing Complexity: While core plans may mention 'unlimited agents,' Sales CRM roles are explicitly priced at ₹499 (~$6.01 USD) per agent per month, with a minimum of 5 agents, segmenting user pricing for sales-focused teams.
- Custom Field/Tag/Event Limits: The number of custom fields (15-30), tags (15-45), and custom events (0-7) is capped on lower-tier plans, becoming unlimited only at the Enterprise tier.
- Messaging Markup on Lower Tiers: Despite the Enterprise plan's claim of 'No Templates Markups,' the listed per-message costs for Growth and Advanced plans (e.g., Marketing ₹0.958) suggest a markup exists for non-Enterprise customers.
- Post-Reply Flow Limitations: Basic post-reply options like 'Opt-Out Flow' are available on the Starter plan, but more advanced interactive options and workflows (especially branched workflows) are gated behind Growth and Advanced plans.
- Template Type Limitations: Richer interactive template types, including Carousel, WhatsApp Form, and WhatsApp Pay options, are only available from the Growth plan onwards, with the Starter plan limited to basic button templates.

## Objection Handling

### Interakt offers unlimited agents, so it's a better value than Wati which caps users.
**Response:** While Interakt advertises 'unlimited agents' for general use, its Sales CRM roles incur an additional ₹499 (~$6.01) per agent per month, starting with a minimum of 5 agents, complicating the true 'unlimited' claim. Wati provides transparent, fixed user counts for its core plans with clear pricing for additional users, ensuring predictable costs.

**Proof points:** Interakt: 'Unlimited agents (All Roles) *Sales CRM Roles not included*'; Sales CRM plan: '₹499/agent (per month +taxes) (min. 5 agents)', Wati Growth: $59 (3 users, hard cap); Pro: $119 (5 users + $39/extra)

### Interakt supports Instagram and RCS, making it a truly omnichannel platform, unlike Wati.
**Response:** Interakt does provide broader channel support, which is beneficial for businesses seeking a unified inbox beyond WhatsApp. However, Wati is an official Meta and Google partner, offering a highly optimized and robust solution specifically for WhatsApp API, which is the primary focus for many businesses' communication strategies.

**Proof points:** Interakt: Offers WhatsApp, Instagram & RCS channels., Wati: 'official Meta + Google partner'

### Interakt boasts specialized AI agents for various functions, which seems more advanced than Wati's general AI agent.
**Response:** Interakt offers several pre-built AI agents (e.g., Sales, Booking, Support) as an add-on cost of $74.99/month. Wati also offers a native AI agent (Astra) that, while having an upsell cost of ~$100/month, is deeply integrated and provides a 'Bring Your Own Assistant' (BYOA) framework for greater customization and control over your AI strategy.

**Proof points:** Interakt: 'Add on: WhatsApp AI Agents @$74.99 for Growth & Advance plan'., Wati: 'native AI agent upsells to Astra at ~$100/mo', 'BYOA framework'

### Interakt's pricing is much lower than Wati's, even for advanced features and many users.
**Response:** While Interakt's base plan pricing appears lower, it's listed in INR and has a modular add-on structure for AI and Sales CRM agents that can increase overall costs. Wati offers transparent USD pricing with clear inclusions, and its official Meta + Google partner status and SOC 2 compliance provide significant value in terms of reliability, security, and proven industry standing.

**Proof points:** Interakt Growth: ₹2499 (~$30.11 USD), AI add-on $74.99 USD. Wati Growth: $59 USD., Wati: 'SOC 2', 'official Meta + Google partner'

### Interakt offers advanced marketing features like click and conversion tracking that Wati lacks, hindering my campaign optimization.
**Response:** Interakt indeed offers specific click and conversion tracking via custom events, which is a strong feature for granular marketing analytics. Wati currently focuses on robust core campaign delivery and automation, and while it lacks native revenue attribution or A/B testing, businesses can integrate external analytics tools for deeper insights.

**Proof points:** Interakt: 'Click Tracking (Templates sent in Campaigns)', 'Conversion Tracking via Custom Events (Campaigns)'., Wati: 'no revenue attribution or A/B testing'

### I need integrated payment options within WhatsApp, which Interakt offers but Wati does not.
**Response:** Interakt's native payment integrations for WhatsApp are a compelling feature for streamlined commerce. While Wati does not offer these direct in-app integrations, it provides robust API capabilities, allowing businesses to seamlessly connect their preferred payment gateways or existing e-commerce systems through custom integrations.

**Proof points:** Interakt: 'Native payments', 'Accept Payments Within WhatsApp with WhatsApp Pay, UPI, Net Banking, Cards, PayU, Instamojo, and Razorpay'., Wati: Integrations are a general strength, allowing flexibility with payment systems (BYOA framework also implies this for deeper tech).

## Frequently Asked Questions

How to get WhatsApp Business API?

To get access to the WhatsApp Business API, you need to apply through a WhatsApp Business Solution Provider (BSP) like Interakt. The process involves verifying your business, setting up a WhatsApp Business Account (WABA), and linking it to a phone number. Once approved, you can start using WhatsApp API for customer engagement.

How to integrate WhatsApp Business API?

Integrating the WhatsApp Business API requires setting up a WhatsApp Business Account, verifying your phone number, and connecting it to a messaging platform like Interakt. You can integrate it with CRM, e-commerce platforms, or third-party tools via APIs or pre-built connectors for seamless automation.

How to create a WhatsApp Business API account?

To create a WhatsApp Business API account, follow these steps:

1.   Register your business with Meta Business Manager.

Apply for API access via a WhatsApp BSP like Interakt.
2.   Verify your business and phone number.
3.   Set up message templates and integrate with your preferred platform.

Once approved, you can start using the API for customer interactions.

Can I download WhatsApp Business API?

No, the WhatsApp Business API is not an app you can download. It is a cloud-based or on-premise solution that requires integration with a business platform like Interakt. You need to set it up through an official WhatsApp BSP to use it for customer messaging.

Can I use WhatsApp API for sending messages in bulk?

Yes, businesses can send bulk messages using the WhatsApp Business API, but all messages must comply with WhatsApp’s policies. You must use pre-approved message templates for outbound notifications, while session messages (customer-initiated) allow more flexibility.

Is WhatsApp Cloud API similar to WhatsApp Business API?

Yes, the WhatsApp Cloud API and WhatsApp Business API offer similar functionalities, but with key differences. The Cloud API is hosted by Meta, making it more scalable and easier to set up for someone with coding and tech know-how. The on-premise Business API requires a BSP-managed solution, where a third-party sets-up your APIs.

What is the best WhatsApp Business platform for businesses?

The best WhatsApp Business platform depends on your business needs. If you’re looking for an all-in-one solution, Interakt offers a powerful platform with automation, CRM integrations, campaign management, and analytics to help businesses grow using WhatsApp.

What are the key WhatsApp Business API documentation required to set up an account?

To set up a WhatsApp Business API account, you need:

1.   A verified **Meta Business Manager** account.
2.   A **phone number** that is not linked to another WhatsApp account.
3.   **Business verification documents** – GST or other relevant document
4.   Access to a **WhatsApp BSP** like Interakt for seamless onboarding.

How is WhatsApp Business API pricing calculated?

WhatsApp Business API pricing is based on a conversation-based model, where charges apply per conversation session (24-hour window). Pricing varies based on:

1.   User-initiated vs. business-initiated messages
2.   Category of messages (Marketing, Utility, Authentication, or Service)
3.   Regional pricing based on customer location
4.   Interakt provides a transparent pricing structure to help businesses optimize costs.
