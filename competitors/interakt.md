## TL;DR vs Wati
Interakt positions itself as a comprehensive multi-channel customer engagement platform, offering WhatsApp, Instagram, RCS, and Voice support with advanced AI capabilities and a dedicated Sales CRM. It directly competes with Wati by providing more flexible agent pricing on base plans and broader AI offerings, while Wati maintains its advantage in simplified WhatsApp-first onboarding and core platform reliability.
<!-- /ANCHOR:tldr -->

<!-- ANCHOR:wins -->
## Where We Win
- Wati explicitly offers the 'easiest WhatsApp API onboarding', a claim Interakt doesn't make, though they promise quick setup for specific features.
- Wati is an official 'Meta + Google partner', while Interakt only specifies 'Meta Business Partners' in the provided content.
- Wati provides a unique 'BYOA (Bring Your Own Agent)' framework, offering greater flexibility for integrating custom AI solutions, which Interakt does not feature.
- Wati highlights 'SOC 2' compliance, indicating a stronger emphasis on enterprise-grade security certifications not mentioned by Interakt.
- Wati's message pricing (even with the 20% markup) is transparently listed in USD, whereas Interakt's Growth/Advanced plans list message costs in INR, potentially leading to currency conversion complexities for US customers.
- Wati's core platform pricing structure (per user tiers) might be simpler to understand for some buyers, contrasting with Interakt's 'unlimited agents' for general roles but per-agent cost for Sales CRM roles.
<!-- /ANCHOR:wins -->

<!-- ANCHOR:losses -->
## Where We Lose
- Interakt offers native multi-channel support for Instagram, RCS, and Voice (MyCallGenie, WhatsApp Voice Calling), while Wati is primarily WhatsApp-focused.
- Interakt boasts a wider array of specialized AI Agents (Sales, Support, Booking, Lead Qualification, Product Recommendation, Order Management) and the 'SOLO' AI Business Team, offering more diverse AI functionalities than Wati's general AI agent.
- Interakt provides a dedicated Sales CRM with visual pipeline tracking, lead qualification, and role-based access, directly addressing Wati's weakness in revenue attribution and CRM depth.
- Interakt's base 'Growth' ($55/mo) and 'Advanced' ($69/mo) plans offer 'Unlimited agents' for Marketing & Support Hub roles, significantly surpassing Wati's 3-user hard cap on its Growth plan ($59/mo).
- Interakt's 'Enterprise' plan explicitly states 'No Templates Markups', which could be a significant cost advantage over Wati's ~20% Meta markup.
- Interakt offers a broader range of native payment integrations within WhatsApp (WhatsApp Pay, UPI, Net Banking, Cards, PayU, Instamojo, Razorpay).
- Interakt's Advanced plan includes sophisticated chatbot capabilities like 'branching, API calls & Conditions', enabling more complex automated workflows.
- Interakt explicitly advertises 'Campaign Auto-retries' for undelivered messages, a feature not prominently highlighted by Wati.
<!-- /ANCHOR:losses -->

## Feature Notes (from their docs)
- Interakt's 'Unlimited agents' on Growth and Advanced plans specifically exclude 'Sales CRM Roles', which require a separate add-on charge per agent (approx. $6 USD/agent).
- WhatsApp AI Agents are an add-on priced at $74.99/month for Growth & Advanced plans, indicating they are not included in the base subscription.
- MyCallGenie, the AI call receptionist, is a distinct product priced at $15/month for 100 calls, with additional credits costing $0.15 each.
- Access to the RCS channel is limited to Interakt's 'Enterprise' plan, making it an exclusive feature for top-tier customers.
- Message costs for Interakt's Growth and Advanced plans are listed in Indian Rupees (₹), which may require currency conversion and clarification for non-Indian customers.
- Interakt's plans include specific limits on custom fields (15-30), custom tags (15-45), and custom events (5-7), with unlimited options only available at the Enterprise level.
- Advanced webhooks are only available from the 'Advanced' plan onwards, limiting complex integration options for lower-tier customers.
- The /docs and /features URLs provided returned 404 errors, indicating a lack of accessible detailed product documentation for deeper technical insights or specific API behaviors.

## Objection Handling

### Interakt's base plans are cheaper, and they offer 'unlimited agents' which sounds like better value than Wati's user caps.
**Response:** While Interakt's base plans offer 'unlimited agents' for marketing and support, critical Sales CRM functionalities (like pipeline management and dedicated contact visibility) require separate 'Sales CRM Roles' priced per agent, which significantly increases costs for sales-focused teams. Wati's clear per-user model, while capped, includes core team inbox features for all users.

**Proof points:** Interakt Growth $55/mo, Advanced $69/mo state 'Unlimited agents (All Roles)' with a note: 'Sales CRM Roles not included'., Interakt's Sales CRM plan is priced at ₹499/agent (approx. $6/agent, minimum 5 agents).

### Interakt's AI Agents seem much more advanced and cover more use cases (Sales, Booking, Order Management) than Wati's AI.
**Response:** Wati's native AI agent, Astra, while positioned as an upsell, offers comprehensive conversational AI, and Wati's BYOA framework allows for integrating any advanced third-party AI for highly specialized needs. Wati prioritizes core WhatsApp API reliability and ease of use, ensuring your primary channel is robust, with flexible AI additions.

**Proof points:** Interakt's WhatsApp AI Agents are an add-on at $74.99 for Growth & Advanced plans., Wati's native AI agent upsells to Astra at ~$100/mo.

### Interakt supports Instagram, RCS, and Voice, making it a truly omnichannel solution. Wati seems WhatsApp-only.
**Response:** Wati specializes in WhatsApp, providing the deepest feature set and most stable experience for your most critical conversational commerce channel, including WhatsApp Business Calling. While Interakt offers more channels, the depth of integration and feature stability across all may vary. Wati's official Meta partner status specifically for WhatsApp ensures best-in-class performance for this platform.

**Proof points:** Interakt lists WhatsApp, Instagram, RCS, Voice as supported channels; RCS is only available on Enterprise plan., Wati offers WhatsApp Business Calling as a core feature.

### Interakt explicitly states 'No Templates Markups' on their Enterprise plan, which addresses Wati's 20% Meta markup issue.
**Response:** While Interakt's Enterprise plan may offer no markups, their other plans list message costs in INR, making transparency on effective USD pricing for message markups less clear for non-Indian customers. Wati's 20% Meta markup, while a known weakness, is clearly stated and applies consistently across all plans, allowing for predictable cost calculation.

**Proof points:** Interakt Growth/Advanced plans list message costs in INR (e.g., Marketing ₹0.958)., Interakt's Enterprise plan states 'No Templates Markups', while Wati has a ~20% Meta markup.

### Interakt has a dedicated Sales CRM with pipeline management, which Wati lacks.
**Response:** While Wati doesn't offer a native full-fledged CRM, it integrates seamlessly with many popular CRMs via its robust API, allowing businesses to leverage their existing CRM investments without migrating data. Wati focuses on providing strong messaging and automation features within WhatsApp to feed into your CRM.

**Proof points:** Interakt's Sales CRM offers 'Sales Pipeline Tracking' and is priced at ₹499/agent (approx. $6/agent, min. 5 agents)., Wati lists 'no revenue attribution or A/B testing' as a weakness, implying less native CRM capability.

## Frequently Asked Questions

How to get WhatsApp Business API?

To get access to the WhatsApp Business API, you need to apply through a WhatsApp Business Solution Provider (BSP) like Interakt. The process involves verifying your business, setting up a WhatsApp Business Account (WABA), and linking it to a phone number. Once approved, you can start using WhatsApp API for customer engagement.

How to integrate WhatsApp Business API?

Integrating the WhatsApp Business API requires setting up a WhatsApp Business Account, verifying your phone number, and connecting it to a messaging platform like Interakt. You can integrate it with CRM, e-commerce platforms, or third-party tools via APIs or pre-built connectors for seamless automation.

How to create a WhatsApp Business API account?

To create a WhatsApp Business API account, follow these steps:

1.   Register your business with Meta Business Manager.

Apply for API access via a WhatsApp BSP like Interakt.
2.   Verify your business and phone number.
3.   Set up message templates and integrate with your preferred platform.

Once approved, you can start using the API for customer interactions.

Can I download WhatsApp Business API?

No, the WhatsApp Business API is not an app you can download. It is a cloud-based or on-premise solution that requires integration with a business platform like Interakt. You need to set it up through an official WhatsApp BSP to use it for customer messaging.

Can I use WhatsApp API for sending messages in bulk?

Yes, businesses can send bulk messages using the WhatsApp Business API, but all messages must comply with WhatsApp’s policies. You must use pre-approved message templates for outbound notifications, while session messages (customer-initiated) allow more flexibility.
