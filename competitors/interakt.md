## TL;DR vs Wati
Interakt presents itself as a more comprehensive omnichannel solution than Wati, offering native Instagram, RCS, and Voice support in addition to WhatsApp. While significantly undercutting Wati on core plan pricing and user limits, Interakt's primary strength lies in its robust Sales CRM, advanced AI agents powered by Haptik, and detailed WhatsApp Voice Calling analytics, which directly address several of Wati's known weaknesses.
<!-- /ANCHOR:tldr -->

<!-- ANCHOR:wins -->
## Where We Win
- Wati offers an 'easiest WhatsApp API onboarding' experience, which Interakt also claims but Wati has established this as a core strength.
- Wati is an official Google partner, a partnership Interakt does not explicitly highlight.
- Wati supports a BYOA (Bring Your Own Account) framework, which is not mentioned in Interakt's documentation.
- Wati is SOC 2 compliant, providing a strong security and compliance advantage that Interakt does not feature.
- Wati has a broader established market presence, being strong in SEA, MENA, and LATAM, while Interakt lists specific countries like India, Indonesia, Brazil, UAE, Philippines, and Africa.
- Wati's AI agent, while an upsell to Astra at ~$100/mo, is an established offering, whereas Interakt's AI agent add-on is $74.99/mo and requires base plan.
- Wati's pricing is transparent regarding Meta's 20% markup, whereas Interakt's message costs imply markups on lower tiers but without a clear percentage stated, potentially creating uncertainty for some customers.
<!-- /ANCHOR:wins -->

<!-- ANCHOR:losses -->
## Where We Lose
- Interakt offers multi-channel support including WhatsApp, Instagram, RCS, and Voice, while Wati is primarily focused on WhatsApp.
- Interakt provides a dedicated Sales CRM with pipeline management, role-based access, and detailed sales reports, a feature Wati explicitly lacks (no revenue attribution).
- Interakt's AI Agents are more granular (FAQ, Booking, Lead Qualification, Sales, Product Recommendation, Order Management) and are powered by Haptik's enterprise-grade AI tech, offering deeper AI capabilities than Wati's basic native AI or Astra upsell.
- Interakt's Enterprise plan explicitly states 'No Templates Markups', directly addressing Wati's ~20% Meta markup weakness on template messages.
- Interakt's WhatsApp Voice Calling includes robust features like call recordings, AI transcripts, AI summaries, and agent performance indicators, which are included in Advanced/CRM plans at no extra feature cost (only per-minute charges). Wati only mentions WhatsApp Business Calling without such detail.
- Interakt offers richer messaging and commerce features like WhatsApp Forms, Carousel Templates, and native WhatsApp Pay integration with various gateways (UPI, Net Banking, Cards, PayU, Instamojo, Razorpay), which Wati's marketing pages do not detail.
- Interakt provides advanced campaign features such as Click Tracking, Conversion Tracking via Custom Events, and Campaign Auto-retries, which are not highlighted in Wati's documentation.
- Interakt allows 'Unlimited agents' for core Marketing & Support Hub features on its Growth and Advanced plans, significantly more flexible than Wati's hard user caps.
- Interakt boasts a larger claimed customer base of '50,000+ Businesses Globally' compared to Wati's '16K+ customers'.
- Interakt's base plan pricing is significantly lower than Wati's across comparable tiers, offering much greater value on a per-user or feature basis.

## Feature Notes (from their docs)
- Interakt's Growth, Advanced, and Enterprise plans include 'Unlimited agents (All Roles)' for Marketing & Support features, but 'Sales CRM Roles not included' and require an additional Sales CRM plan ($4.81/agent/month annually, minimum 5 agents).
- Custom fields, tags, and events have limits based on the plan: Starter (15/15/NA), Growth (25/30/5), Advanced (30/45/7), Enterprise (Unlimited).
- WhatsApp AI Agents are an add-on at $74.99/month for Growth & Advanced plans.
- WhatsApp Voice Calling is bundled with Advanced & Sales CRM plans; it includes call recordings, AI transcripts, and AI summaries at no additional feature cost (only per-minute charges apply).
- Message costs for Marketing, Authentication, and Utility templates are explicitly listed in INR for Growth and Advanced plans (e.g., Marketing ₹0.958), implying a markup, unlike the Enterprise plan which has 'No Templates Markups'.
- Click-to-WhatsApp Ads conversion data can be passed to Meta from the Growth plan upwards, offering better campaign optimization.
- WhatsApp Forms are available from the Starter plan for creation, but sending capabilities (e.g., via branched workflows) are restricted to higher tiers like Advanced.
- Chat auto-assignment with Load Equalizer and assignment based on Custom Fields is available from the Advanced plan.
- Interactive Messaging Templates can have 'Upto 10 Buttons, Send Products/Catalog, Limited Time Offer, Carousel, WhatsApp Form, WhatsApp Pay' from the Growth plan.
- Interakt provides mobile apps for both iOS and Android platforms, indicated by footer links to respective app stores.

## Objection Handling

### Interakt offers more channels like Instagram and RCS, making it a better all-in-one solution.
**Response:** While Interakt offers Instagram and RCS, Wati specializes in WhatsApp Business API with a focus on compliance and robust features specifically tailored for WhatsApp, which remains the dominant channel in key markets. We prioritize depth and reliability on WhatsApp, an official Meta partner.

**Proof points:** Interakt lists Instagram, RCS, and Voice alongside WhatsApp., Wati is an official Meta partner, Interakt is also a Meta Business Partner.

### Interakt's pricing is much lower, and they offer 'unlimited agents' on their core plans.
**Response:** Wati's pricing includes a dedicated number of users with clear caps, ensuring consistent performance and support. While Interakt offers 'unlimited agents' for general use, specialized Sales CRM roles come at an additional cost per agent, and Wati ensures transparent costs from the outset.

**Proof points:** Interakt Growth plan: ~$24.09/month (unlimited agents, non-CRM)., Interakt Sales CRM Plan: ~$4.81/agent/month, min 5 agents.

### Interakt's AI agents are more advanced and can handle more specific tasks like sales, bookings, and order management.
**Response:** Wati's native AI agent, Astra, integrates seamlessly for general support and automation needs, and we ensure it aligns with our user-friendly onboarding. Interakt's AI might be broader but requires an additional $74.99/month, making Wati's streamlined approach competitive on value.

**Proof points:** Interakt AI Agents add-on: $74.99/month., Wati native AI agent (Astra) upsells at ~$100/month.

### Interakt offers a full Sales CRM with pipeline management and reports, which Wati doesn't have.
**Response:** Wati focuses on providing a powerful WhatsApp API platform for communication, which integrates with existing CRM tools for sales pipeline management. We prioritize robust messaging capabilities and ease of integration over building a native CRM, allowing customers flexibility to use their preferred sales tools.

**Proof points:** Interakt has a dedicated 'Sales CRM' product with pipeline tracking and reports., Wati's weaknesses include 'no revenue attribution or A/B testing'.

### Interakt's Enterprise plan offers 'No Templates Markups', unlike Wati's 20% Meta markup.
**Response:** Wati is transparent about the Meta markup, ensuring customers understand the true cost structure. For enterprise clients requiring significant message volumes, Wati can explore custom solutions to optimize messaging costs, while prioritizing an official and compliant Meta partner experience.

**Proof points:** Interakt Enterprise plan: 'No Templates Markups'., Wati weakness: '~20% Meta markup (highest)'.

### Interakt provides WhatsApp Voice Calling with recordings, AI transcripts, and summaries.
**Response:** Wati provides reliable WhatsApp Business Calling. While Interakt's additional analytics are detailed, Wati focuses on enabling direct voice communication for sales and support teams, offering a straightforward calling feature that integrates into the shared inbox.

**Proof points:** Interakt WhatsApp Voice Calling includes 'Recordings, AI transcripts, AI summaries, Agent Performance Indicators'., Wati has 'WhatsApp Business Calling'.
