## TL;DR vs Wati
Interakt is a multi-channel customer engagement platform that directly challenges Wati by offering integrated WhatsApp, Instagram, and RCS support, coupled with advanced AI capabilities including specialized AI agents and an AI call receptionist. Their pricing model featuring 'unlimited agents' in core plans and bundled, feature-rich WhatsApp Voice Calling presents a strong value proposition, especially for SMBs and growing teams seeking broader channel support and more aggressive agent pricing.

## Where We Win
- Wati is an official Google Partner, a credential Interakt does not explicitly claim.
- Wati holds SOC 2 compliance, a critical security certification that is not mentioned in Interakt's provided content.
- Wati boasts the 'easiest WhatsApp API onboarding,' emphasizing a streamlined setup process (Interakt advertises '3 Steps' but not 'easiest').
- Wati supports a 'BYOA framework,' offering greater flexibility for custom integrations and infrastructure control not explicitly advertised by Interakt.
- Wati offers a simpler, consolidated platform where WhatsApp Business Calling is a native feature, whereas Interakt markets 'MyCallGenie AI Call Receptionist' as a separate, distinct product with its own pricing.
- Wati's pricing model, despite user caps, may be easier for some smaller teams to understand upfront due to its clear, inclusive user tiers versus Interakt's 'unlimited agents' with exclusions for Sales CRM roles.
- Wati's mobile app is noted as 'sluggish on Android,' but Interakt's mobile app performance (for SOLO or main platform) is not detailed enough to guarantee a superior experience, making Wati's mobile experience a potential point of parity rather than a clear loss.
- Wati's 16K+ customers, while smaller than Interakt's claimed 50K+, represent a dedicated base for its focused WhatsApp solution.

## Where We Lose
- Interakt offers 'Unlimited agents (All Roles)' in its Growth ($55/mo annual) and Advanced ($69/mo annual) plans (excluding Sales CRM roles), providing significantly better scalability for agents compared to Wati's rigid 3-user cap on its Growth plan and per-user charges.
- Interakt's WhatsApp AI Agents add-on is priced at $74.99/month, which is more affordable than Wati's native AI agent upsell to Astra at ~$100/month.
- Interakt includes WhatsApp Business Voice Calling with advanced features like call recordings, AI transcripts, and AI summaries for free (only per-minute charges) in its Advanced and CRM plans, a feature set that Interakt explicitly positions as superior to Wati's offering.
- Interakt offers robust conversion tracking, including 'Conversion Tracking via Custom Events (Campaigns)' and the ability to 'Pass Conversion Data to Meta,' addressing Wati's weakness in lacking revenue attribution and A/B testing.
- Interakt provides broader multi-channel support with native integrations for Instagram (DMs & comments, automation) and RCS (Enterprise plan), extending beyond Wati's primary focus on WhatsApp.
- Interakt's Enterprise plan explicitly offers 'No Templates Markups,' providing a significant cost advantage for high-volume users compared to Wati's stated '~20% Meta markup (highest)' across its plans.
- Interakt's Advanced plan includes 'advanced chatbot flows with branching, API calls & Conditions,' indicating more sophisticated no-code automation capabilities than Wati's standard chatbot builder.
- Interakt supports native payments within WhatsApp (WhatsApp Pay, UPI, Net Banking, Cards, PayU, Instamojo, Razorpay) and 'WhatsApp Commerce' features like product catalogs, streamlining the e-commerce conversion funnel.
- Interakt boasts a significantly larger reported customer base of '50,000+ Businesses Globally' compared to Wati's '16K+ customers'.
- Interakt offers a dedicated 'Sales CRM' product with pipeline tracking, lead auto-assignment, and role-based access, providing more specialized sales management features than Wati's general team inbox.

## Objection Handling

### Interakt seems to offer unlimited users at a lower price, making Wati's per-user pricing less competitive.
**Response:** While Interakt offers 'unlimited agents' in its Growth and Advanced plans, this explicitly excludes dedicated Sales CRM roles, which incur additional costs per agent. Wati's plans provide clear, inclusive user access up to specified caps, ensuring predictable total costs for teams utilizing all features within their chosen tier.

**Proof points:** Interakt's Pricing page: 'Unlimited agents (All Roles) Sales CRM Roles not included.', Interakt's Sales CRM page: '₹499/agent (per month +taxes) (min. 5 agents)' for CRM roles.

### Interakt's AI agent add-on is cheaper at $74.99/month compared to Wati's Astra AI at ~$100/month.
**Response:** Interakt's AI agent add-on is indeed more affordable. However, Wati also offers foundational AI automation capabilities within its core platform. The Astra AI upsell is for advanced, higher-tier AI capabilities, and Wati emphasizes an integrated platform experience where AI complements core features.

**Proof points:** Interakt: 'Add on: WhatsApp AI Agents @$74.99 for Growth & Advance plan.', Wati context: 'native AI agent upsells to Astra at ~$100/mo.'

### Interakt includes WhatsApp Voice Calling with AI summaries and recordings for free (only per-minute charges), which Wati doesn't seem to offer at that level.
**Response:** Wati also provides WhatsApp Business Calling, ensuring direct customer communication. While Interakt bundles advanced voice features like AI transcripts and summaries into its higher plans, Wati's focus is on a streamlined, integrated messaging and calling experience within its core platform, without splitting out an 'AI Call Receptionist' as a separate product.

**Proof points:** Interakt's WhatsApp Voice Calling page: 'WhatsApp Business Voice Calling is included by default on Advanced & CRM plans! Only pay competitive per-minute calling charges.', Interakt's comparison table on WhatsApp Voice Calling page: Interakt includes 'CRM-integrated call logs' and 'Call recordings & transcripts' for free, while Wati shows '❌ No' or '⚠️ Paid add-ons'.

### Interakt has 'no templates markups' on their Enterprise plan, implying lower overall messaging costs compared to Wati's stated ~20% Meta markup.
**Response:** Interakt's 'No Templates Markups' is a significant benefit, but it's exclusive to their Enterprise plan. This implies that markups likely apply to their other plans, though the exact percentage is not disclosed. Wati, on the other hand, provides transparency by openly stating its ~20% Meta markup across all plans from the outset.

**Proof points:** Interakt's Pricing page: 'Enterprise: No Templates Markups.', Wati context: '~20% Meta markup (highest).'

### Interakt offers Instagram and RCS support, giving me more channels for communication than Wati.
**Response:** Wati's core strength is its deep specialization and proven expertise in the WhatsApp Business API, delivering a robust and optimized experience for the world's most popular messaging app. While Interakt offers broader multi-channel support, Wati ensures the most reliable and easy-to-use WhatsApp solution, which remains the primary channel for many businesses.

**Proof points:** Interakt's Homepage/Pricing: Supports WhatsApp, Instagram, and RCS (Enterprise only)., Wati context: Predominantly focused on WhatsApp Business Solution.

### Interakt has a dedicated Sales CRM and advanced conversion tracking, which Wati lacks.
**Response:** Interakt does offer a dedicated Sales CRM and detailed conversion tracking features. While Wati currently lacks native revenue attribution, its robust API and integration capabilities allow customers to seamlessly connect with their existing CRM systems and analytical tools to achieve comprehensive tracking and sales management.

**Proof points:** Interakt's Marketing Hub: 'Conversion Tracking via Custom Events (Campaigns)' and 'CTWA Ads – Pass Conversion Data to Meta'., Wati context: 'no revenue attribution or A/B testing.'

## Frequently Asked Questions

How to get WhatsApp Business API?

To get access to the WhatsApp Business API, you need to apply through a WhatsApp Business Solution Provider (BSP) like Interakt. The process involves verifying your business, setting up a WhatsApp Business Account (WABA), and linking it to a phone number. Once approved, you can start using WhatsApp API for customer engagement.

How to integrate WhatsApp Business API?

Integrating the WhatsApp Business API requires setting up a WhatsApp Business Account, verifying your phone number, and connecting it to a messaging platform like Interakt. You can integrate it with CRM, e-commerce platforms, or third-party tools via APIs or pre-built connectors for seamless automation.

How to create a WhatsApp Business API account?

To create a WhatsApp Business API account, follow these steps:

1.   Register your business with Meta Business Manager.

Apply for API access via a WhatsApp BSP like Interakt.
2.   Verify your business and phone number.
3.   Set up message templates and integrate with your preferred platform.

Once approved, you can start using the API for customer interactions.

Can I download WhatsApp Business API?

No, the WhatsApp Business API is not an app you can download. It is a cloud-based or on-premise solution that requires integration with a business platform like Interakt. You need to set it up through an official WhatsApp BSP to use it for customer messaging.

Can I use WhatsApp API for sending messages in bulk?

Yes, businesses can send bulk messages using the WhatsApp Business API, but all messages must comply with WhatsApp’s policies. You must use pre-approved message templates for outbound notifications, while session messages (customer-initiated) allow more flexibility.

Is WhatsApp Cloud API similar to WhatsApp Business API?

Yes, the WhatsApp Cloud API and WhatsApp Business API offer similar functionalities, but with key differences. The Cloud API is hosted by Meta, making it more scalable and easier to set up for someone with coding and tech know-how. The on-premise Business API requires a BSP-managed solution, where a third-party sets-up your APIs.

What is the best WhatsApp Business platform for businesses?

The best WhatsApp Business platform depends on your business needs. If you’re looking for an all-in-one solution, Interakt offers a powerful platform with automation, CRM integrations, campaign management, and analytics to help businesses grow using WhatsApp.

What are the key WhatsApp Business API documentation required to set up an account?

To set up a WhatsApp Business API account, you need:

1.   A verified **Meta Business Manager** account.
2.   A **phone number** that is not linked to another WhatsApp account.
3.   **Business verification documents** – GST or other relevant document
4.   Access to a **WhatsApp BSP** like Interakt for seamless onboarding.

How is WhatsApp Business API pricing calculated?

WhatsApp Business API pricing is based on a conversation-based model, where charges apply per conversation session (24-hour window). Pricing varies based on:

1.   User-initiated vs. business-initiated messages
2.   Category of messages (Marketing, Utility, Authentication, or Service)
3.   Regional pricing based on customer location
4.   Interakt provides a transparent pricing structure to help businesses optimize costs.
